The evidence boundary
The current investigation distinguishes a verified numerical recovery from a validated physical prediction. The recovered
electron-history diagnostics and integration checks are reproducible technical results, but they do not establish that the
full Thomson optical depth, the CMB temperature and polarization spectra, or cosmological likelihood values are accurate
enough to change model comparisons.


What remains unresolved
The optical-depth reference-truth gate remains UNQUALIFIED and the final physical-materiality result remains
UNRESOLVED. No new observation, Hubble-constant estimate, early-dark-energy preference, or likelihood-portability
classification follows. The discrepancy between local and early-universe H0 inference chains remains open, as does the
Planck likelihood-implementation tension.
