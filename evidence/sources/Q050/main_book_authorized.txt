What Q050 establishes
The exact low-redshift source-real hydrogen/temperature selector now has a global existence theorem in the
declared fixed-background domain. For each admitted common background, both held-helium values q=10^-6 and
q=0, and every validated z=810 theorem-domain start, at least one absolutely continuous Caratheodory continuation
reaches z=0 without changing or convexifying the selector.
The proof closes the previously isolated PEEBLES and Pion selector gates, keeps the inherited
hydrogen/temperature invariant region, handles fixed-time dispatch and reionization, and shows that even a
finite-time accumulation of selector switches cannot terminate existence: bounded branch fields give an endpoint
limit from which the local literal solution can restart.
What Q050 does not establish
Q050 is an existence result, not a uniqueness result. It does not prove the admitted background itself, identify every
theorem-domain state as cosmologically reachable, certify the native/P6 floating-point trajectory or establish a new
observation. The full four-configuration Q048 electron-history and optical-depth comparison therefore remains
unresolved.
Why Q049 is still not a small-effect result
Q049's finite bound |Delta tau_T,tail| <= 46.67050453 remains deliberately coarse. Q050 removes the open
existence premise, but the bound still allows order-unity electron-history differences and therefore does not show
that the actual residual-helium effect is small.
